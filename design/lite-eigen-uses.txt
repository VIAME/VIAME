## The wider surface

`git grep Eigen::` finds only the lines that name it. Vital's math typedefs
-- `vector_3d`, `matrix_3x3d`, `rotation_d`, `covariance_2d` and the rest --
are Eigen types under other names, so every line that uses one depends on
Eigen's operators without saying so. Counting those as well:

  96 files under library/, tools/ and plugins/ touch Eigen or one of the
  typedefs (excluding plugins/pytorch/learn, which vendors third-party python
  packages that carry their own copy and become wheels in phase 9)

  847 lines name a typedef

    plugins/core                     15

The member functions those files actually call on a math type, which is the
real specification for `core_types/math`:

  dot, transpose, inverse, setZero, setIdentity, normalized, normalize, norm,
  squaredNorm, determinant, cross, head, block, hnormalized

plus the operators, `Identity()`, `Zero()`, `Random()` in one test, and the
four decompositions in the list above. Nothing reaches for a sparse type, a
solver, a geometry module beyond `Quaternion`, `AngleAxis` and `AlignedBox`,
or an expression template that is not a block or a cast.
